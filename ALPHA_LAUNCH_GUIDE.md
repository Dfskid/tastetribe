# TasteTribe Alpha Launch Guide

## Welcome Alpha Testers!

Thank you for joining the TasteTribe alpha program. This guide will help you get started and provide important information about what to expect during the alpha phase.

## What is TasteTribe?

TasteTribe is a social food discovery app that helps you find amazing restaurants through friend recommendations. Instead of relying on anonymous reviews, you get personalized suggestions based on people you actually know and trust.

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Create Your Account
1. Visit [https://tastetribe.app](https://tastetribe.app)
2. Click "Sign Up"
3. Enter your email and create a password
4. Verify your email (check spam folder if needed)

### Step 2: Complete Your Profile
1. Add your display name
2. Upload a profile picture (optional but recommended)
3. Set your location for local recommendations

### Step 3: Rate Your Favorite Restaurants
1. Search for restaurants you've visited
2. Rate them on a 1-5 scale
3. Add optional reviews and tags
4. Rate at least 5 restaurants to get started

**Pro Tip**: The more restaurants you rate, the better your recommendations!

### Step 4: Invite Friends
1. Click "Invite Friends" in the navigation
2. Send invites via email or SMS
3. Your friends will receive a personalized invite code
4. Once they join, you'll see their ratings and recommendations

**Goal**: Try to invite at least 3-5 friends during the alpha

### Step 5: Discover New Places
1. Visit the "Discover" page
2. See recommendations from your friends
3. Filter by cuisine, price range, or location
4. Save favorites for later

## Key Features to Test

### 1. Restaurant Ratings
- **How**: Search for a restaurant, click to view details, add your rating
- **Test**: Rate at least 10 restaurants in your first week
- **Feedback**: Is the rating process intuitive? Are the search results accurate?

### 2. Friend Invitations
- **How**: Use the invite system to bring friends onboard
- **Test**: Invite 3-5 friends and help them get started
- **Feedback**: Is the invitation process smooth? Do invites reach friends successfully?

### 3. Recommendations
- **How**: Check the Discover page after you and friends have rated restaurants
- **Test**: Do the recommendations make sense? Do they match your taste?
- **Feedback**: Quality of recommendations, relevance, diversity

### 4. Social Features
- **How**: View friend activity, see what they're rating
- **Test**: Follow friend activity, interact with their ratings
- **Feedback**: Is the social feed engaging? What features are missing?

### 5. Search & Filters
- **How**: Use search to find specific restaurants or cuisines
- **Test**: Try different search queries, apply filters
- **Feedback**: Are results relevant? Do filters work as expected?

## Known Issues

### Current Limitations
1. **No Mobile App**: Web-only for now (mobile-responsive design)
2. **Limited Categories**: Restaurants only (movies coming soon)
3. **Basic Notifications**: Email only, no push notifications yet
4. **Location Services**: Manual location entry required

### Reported Bugs (Being Fixed)
- Search may be slow with large result sets
- Some profile images may not load immediately
- Friend requests occasionally need page refresh
- Maps integration coming in next update

### FAQ

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, check your email for reset link.

**Q: My friend didn't receive the invite. What should I do?**
A: Check if they checked their spam folder. If still missing, contact support with their email.

**Q: Can I delete my account?**
A: Yes, go to Settings > Privacy > Delete Account. This is permanent.

**Q: How is my data used?**
A: See our [Privacy Policy](https://tastetribe.app/privacy). We never sell your data.

**Q: Can I invite friends who aren't in my city?**
A: Yes! The app works best with local friends, but you can connect with anyone.

**Q: What happens after the alpha?**
A: Alpha testers get lifetime benefits and early access to new features.

## Success Metrics

We're tracking these key metrics during alpha:

- **Viral Coefficient**: Average invites sent per user (target: 1.2+)
- **Friend Acceptance Rate**: % of invites accepted (target: 50%+)
- **User Retention**: % of users active after 1 week (target: 40%+)
- **Ratings per User**: Average ratings submitted (target: 10+)
- **Time to First Friend**: How quickly users invite friends (target: <24 hours)

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Problem**: Page won't load
- **Solution**: Clear browser cache, try incognito mode, check internet connection

**Problem**: Can't find a restaurant
- **Solution**: Try different search terms, check spelling, report missing restaurants

**Problem**: Recommendations seem off
- **Solution**: Rate more restaurants (need at least 10), invite more friends

**Problem**: Friends not showing up
- **Solution**: Make sure they accepted the friendship, refresh the page

**Problem**: Slow performance
- **Solution**: Check your internet, try a different browser, report if persists
